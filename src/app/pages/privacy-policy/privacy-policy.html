By providing a phone number to Right Ladder Technologies or through our integrated software applications, users and patients agree to receive automated messages, alerts, and appointment reminders via WhatsApp.

  • Explicit Consent: We only send WhatsApp messages to individuals who have given clear permission to receive them.
  • Opt-Out: Anyone can stop receiving WhatsApp messages at any time by replying with the word "STOP" or by contacting our team.
